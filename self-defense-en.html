About the Course

The Self-Defense Course is a practical program designed to teach participants the fundamentals of personal protection, increase self-confidence, improve quick decision-making, and develop discipline through safe, hands-on training.

🥋 Course Content

Self-defense fundamentals, protection techniques, body control, and responding effectively in different situations.

👥 Target Audience

Youth and teenagers interested in learning useful practical skills.

📅 Schedule

5:00 PM – 6:15 PM

Develop focus, quick decision-making, and personal discipline.
