Yaqini Banquet

A weekly gathering that combines brotherhood, learning, and enjoyable activities.

About the Program

Yaqini Banquet is a weekly program where participants prepare and share a meal together. The gathering includes reading a beneficial book, followed by educational and recreational activities in a faith-filled atmosphere that strengthens friendship, cooperation, and brotherhood.

📅 Schedule

Every Monday
10:00 AM – 1:00 PM

👥 Target Audience

Youth and Teenagers

📍 Location

Yaqini Center

Program Activities

🍽️ Banquet

We gather around a meal prepared by the participants, enjoying an atmosphere of teamwork, friendship, and cooperation.

📚 Book Reading

A session dedicated to reading a beneficial book and discussing its most valuable ideas in an interactive way.

🎲 Activities

Educational group games and activities that strengthen brotherhood, teamwork, and create an enjoyable, energetic atmosphere.
